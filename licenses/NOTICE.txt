dsp.so statically links code from gearmulator (https://github.com/dsp56300/gearmulator,
built from the fork at https://github.com/charlesvestal/gearmulator), which is
licensed under GPL-3.0 with no "or any later version" grant. That makes the
module as a whole GPL-3.0-only. Gearmulator libraries linked: dsp56300
(DSP56300 emulator), baseLib, synthLib, hardwareLib, mc68k, waldi wLib and
microq/mqLib.

Corresponding source: this repository at the release tag, with its
libs/gearmulator submodule (and that submodule's dsp56300, mc68k and asmjit
submodules) at the commits the tag records.
